FAQ

Common questions answered

NEET UG counselling is the process of seat allotment for MBBS/BDS/AYUSH based on your NEET rank. MCC conducts AIQ counselling (15% seats). State authorities handle State Quota (85% seats). It involves registration, choice filling, seat allotment, and document verification — in 3 rounds typically. EduTrack guides you through every single step.
Essential documents: NEET admit card, scorecard, Class 10 & 12 marksheets, Aadhar card, category certificate (OBC/SC/ST/EWS if applicable), domicile proof for state quota, passport-size photos, and PWD certificate if applicable. EduTrack provides a personalized document checklist for every student.
All India Quota (AIQ) covers 15% of MBBS seats in government colleges, open to all states, managed by MCC. State Quota covers 85% of seats — only for students domiciled in that state. EduTrack provides strategy for both simultaneously to maximize your seat options.
During active counselling rounds, we respond within hours on WhatsApp (8484098904). Outside counselling season, responses are within 24 hours. We also take calls directly — 8484098904 (primary) and 8080901797 (alternate).
NRI Quota is 15% of seats in private/deemed medical colleges reserved for NRI students, OCI/PIO cardholders, and Indian students sponsored by NRI relatives. NEET UG qualifying score is mandatory. Fees are higher (typically USD 22,000–80,000/year) but competition is lower. EduTrack specialises in NRI documentation, college selection, and the complete NRI quota admission process. Read the full NRI Quota guide →
Institutional Quota (IQ) is 15% of seats in private medical colleges that the institution fills directly — outside state merit counselling. In Maharashtra, this means ~22 IQ seats per 150-seat college. The key advantage: NO domicile restriction. Students from Bihar, UP, Rajasthan, or any state can get a Maharashtra MBBS seat through IQ. NEET qualifying score is required. Fees are ₹12–22 Lakhs/year. EduTrack has direct college contacts for IQ admissions. Read the full IQ guide →
