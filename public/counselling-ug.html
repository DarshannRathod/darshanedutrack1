How it works

Two parallel counselling streams

After NEET UG results, two counselling processes run simultaneously. You can participate in both — AIQ and your state — to maximise chances.

🏛️

MCC AIQ Counselling

15% of Government MBBS seats — All India Open

Conducted by Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) at mcc.nic.in. Open to students from all states. No domicile restriction for AIQ seats. Includes Govt, Central University, ESIC, AFMC colleges.

🗺️

State Quota Counselling

85% of Government + All Private seats — Domicile Required

Conducted by individual state authorities. You must have state domicile to participate. Each state has its own portal, timeline and category rules. EduTrack manages both AIQ + State strategy simultaneously for you.

Step by Step

Complete NEET UG Counselling Roadmap

EduTrack guides you through every single step — from NEET result day to final college reporting. No confusion, no missed deadlines.

1

NEET UG Result — Check AIR & Category Rank

After results, note your All India Rank (AIR), Category Rank, State Rank and Percentile. All four numbers matter for different quota strategies.

💡 EduTrack Tip: Share your rank with us on WhatsApp immediately — we build your college list within 24 hours.
2

Document Preparation & Verification

Gather and self-attest all required documents. Category certificates (OBC/SC/ST/EWS), domicile, income certificate — must be verified BEFORE registration opens.

💡 Missing a certificate on deadline day = losing a round. EduTrack provides category-specific checklists.
3

MCC AIQ Registration (mcc.nic.in)

Register on the MCC portal, pay the registration fee (₹1,000 General / ₹500 SC/ST/PH), upload documents and verify. Deadline is typically 7-10 days after results.

💡 Register on Day 1 of registration opening — portal sometimes slows down near deadlines.
4

State Portal Registration (Parallel)

Simultaneously register on your state counselling portal. Each state has its own fee, documents and timeline. EduTrack manages both registrations for you.

5

Strategic Choice Filling — The Most Critical Step

Fill your college preference list in optimal order. Wrong ordering = losing a better seat. EduTrack builds your AIQ + State choice list simultaneously, based on 3-year cutoff analysis.

💡 Add 500–1000 choices. More choices = better safety net. Quality of order matters more than quantity.
6

Round 1 Seat Allotment — Accept, Upgrade or Resign?

Check your allotment. Three options: (a) Accept & freeze — no further upgradation, (b) Accept & upgrade — keep seat but try for better in Round 2, (c) Resign — only if you have a better seat elsewhere. EduTrack advises the best move in real time.

💡 Never resign without a confirmed backup. "Accept & Upgrade" is the safest move in most cases.
7

Mop-up & Stray Vacancy Rounds

After Round 2, vacant seats go to Mop-up and Stray Vacancy rounds. Cutoffs often drop significantly. These rounds are golden for students who missed in earlier rounds.

8

Final Reporting at College

Pay the semester/annual fee online, report to the allotted college with original documents, and complete joining formalities within the deadline. EduTrack prepares you completely for this step.

💡 Carry at least 10 sets of all documents. Some colleges ask for more copies than expected.
State-wise Authorities

NEET UG Counselling Authorities Across India

Each state conducts its own counselling for 85% state quota seats. Know your state authority, portal and helpline.

Maharashtra

DMER Maharashtra

Directorate of Medical Education & Research. Controls all MH Govt + Private MBBS/BDS/AYUSH seats. State quota requires MH domicile.

dmer.org / mahacet.org
Uttar Pradesh

DGME Uttar Pradesh

Directorate General of Medical Education, UP. Manages all UP Govt + Private MBBS seats. Large seat matrix — one of India's biggest.

upneet.gov.in
Karnataka

KEA Karnataka

Karnataka Examinations Authority. Standardised 2-tier fee system. Non-Karnataka students can apply for Management/NRI quota.

kea.kar.nic.in
Tamil Nadu

TNMGRMU / DTE Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu Dr. MGR Medical University + DTE. Lowest govt fees in India (₹4,000–₹13,000/yr). Highly competitive for state students.

tnmedicalselection.net
Delhi

DGHS Delhi

Directorate General of Health Services, Delhi. Manages Delhi state quota. Top colleges: MAMC, Lady Hardinge. Extremely competitive ranks.

dghsdelhi.in
Rajasthan

RajMES / RUHS

Rajasthan Medical Education Society. Affordable Govt fees (₹70,340/yr). Expensive private sector. Open states quota available.

rajugneet.in
Kerala

CEE Kerala

Commissioner for Entrance Examinations, Kerala. Lowest private MBBS fees in India. High quality education. Strong domicile verification.

cee.kerala.gov.in
Andhra Pradesh

Dr. NTR UHS

Dr. NTR University of Health Sciences. Manages AP state quota MBBS/BDS. ₹6,000 non-refundable counselling fee (DD to NTR UHS).

ntruhs.ap.nic.in
Telangana

Kaloji NUH / KNRUHS

Kaloji Narayana Rao University of Health Sciences. Manages all Telangana MBBS/BDS counselling including private college seats.

knruhs.telangana.gov.in
West Bengal

WBMCC

West Bengal Medical Counselling Committee. Manages all WB Govt + Private MBBS/BDS counselling. WB domicile required.

wbmcc.nic.in
Gujarat

ACPC Gujarat

Admission Committee for Professional Courses. Manages Gujarat state quota. GJ domicile needed. AIIMS Rajkot under MCC AIQ.

acpc.gujarat.gov.in
All India (AIQ)

MCC — Medical Counselling Committee

Central body for AIQ 15% seats in Govt colleges, AIIMS, JIPMER, ESIC, AFMC. Also manages Deemed and Central University seats.

mcc.nic.in
Document Checklist

Complete Document Requirements for NEET UG Counselling

Missing even one document can cost you a seat. EduTrack provides personalised checklists based on your category and state.

📋 Mandatory for All

  • NEET UG 2026 Admit Card (original)
  • NEET UG 2026 Scorecard / Rank Letter
  • Class 10 Mark Sheet & Certificate (DOB proof)
  • Class 12 Mark Sheet & Passing Certificate
  • Photo ID — Aadhaar Card (original + copies)
  • 8–10 recent passport-size photographs
  • MCC/State counselling registration letter
  • Seat allotment letter (after allotment)

🏷️ Category-specific Documents

  • OBC-NCL certificate (central/state format as applicable)
  • SC/ST certificate (issued by competent authority)
  • EWS certificate (issued in current financial year)
  • PwD (Person with Disability) certificate from CMO
  • Defence ward certificate (if applicable)
  • NRI/OCI passport & proof documents
  • Non-creamy layer certificate for OBC (state level)

🗺️ State Quota Specific

  • Domicile / Residence certificate (state-specific format)
  • State-specific income certificate (if required)
  • Transfer certificate from last school/college
  • Migration certificate (if from another state board)
  • Character certificate from principal
  • Physically challenged certificate from State Medical Board

🏦 For Fee Payment & Reporting

  • Demand Draft / Bank Challan as per college requirement
  • Medical fitness certificate from registered MBBS doctor
  • Gap certificate (if applicable — year gap after 12th)
  • Conduct / Anti-ragging undertaking forms
  • College-specific fee receipt (keep copies)
  • Hostel allotment form (if hostel required)
⚠️

Important: OBC-NCL certificate must be issued by a Tehsildar/SDM and must NOT be older than 3 years for Central / AIQ counselling. EWS certificate must be from the current financial year. EduTrack verifies your documents before every counselling round.
