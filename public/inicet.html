What is INI-CET?

The exam for India's premier medical institutes

INI-CET (Institutes of National Importance Combined Entrance Test) is the PG medical entrance exam conducted jointly by AIIMS New Delhi, JIPMER, PGIMER Chandigarh and NIMHANS. It is separate from NEET PG and gives access to some of the most prestigious PG seats in India.

Conducted twice yearly — May and November sessions
Open to MBBS graduates with completed internship (CRRI)
MD, MS, MDS, M.Sc., DM, MCh seats at participating institutes
Separate exam from NEET PG — requires different preparation strategy
200 MCQ questions from MBBS curriculum — 2.5 hours duration
Negative marking: -1 for wrong answers
INI-CET vs NEET PG

Key differences

INI-CET

• Access: AIIMS, JIPMER, PGIMER, NIMHANS only
• 200 MCQs — CBT mode
• Twice a year (May + November)
• AIIMS-style questions — clinical heavy
• No NEET PG seats via INI-CET

NEET PG

• Access: Govt, private, deemed colleges across India
• 200 MCQs — CBT mode
• Once a year
• Broader syllabus — all MBBS subjects
• 47,000+ MD/MS/DNB seats

EduTrack Tip: Appearing in both INI-CET and NEET PG simultaneously is allowed and highly recommended. Many students get a good seat via one and use the other as a backup.

AIIMS-like Autonomy

All INI-CET institutes are autonomous, centrally funded and have their own rules — no bond service, no state reservation, competitive stipend. High research output and training standards.

Stipend: ₹75,000–1,10,000/month  ·  No bond obligation

Counselling Strategy

How to approach INI-CET counselling

1

Appear in both INI-CET and NEET PG

Most AIIMS-targeting students appear in both. INI-CET is your shot at premier institutes; NEET PG is your safety net. Preparing for one helps the other.

2

Register on the INI-CET counselling portal

After results, register on the AIIMS PG counselling portal — separate from MCC NEET PG portal. Fee and document submission deadlines are strict.

3

Specialty-first or Institute-first?

At AIIMS: specialty-first (Radiology at AIIMS Bhopal over General Medicine at AIIMS Jodhpur?). EduTrack analyses this based on your rank and career goals.

4

Fill choices across all campuses

Don't just aim for AIIMS Delhi. Newer AIIMS campuses still have AIIMS-level training and stipend. Fill all preferred specialty choices across all campuses for maximum allocation chance.

5

Parallel NEET PG strategy

If INI-CET Round 1 doesn't give you the desired seat — don't resign immediately. Check NEET PG allotment simultaneously and decide which to accept.

6

Joining & bond policy

INI-CET seats have no bond service obligation, competitive stipend (₹75K–1.1L/month), and AIIMS-level infrastructure and faculty. Joining formalities are straightforward.
