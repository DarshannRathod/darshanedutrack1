Eligibility

Who can apply for NEET UG?

10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology & English
Min PCB aggregate: General 50% · OBC/SC/ST 40% · PH 45%
Age: 17+ by 31 December of admission year (no upper age limit)
Qualify NEET UG cutoff percentile (varies every year by category)
Indian nationals, NRIs, OCI, PIO and foreign nationals — all eligible

⚠️ Important: Candidates who have appeared in NEET UG but do not fulfil the eligibility criteria of the respective course/college may face cancellation of admission. Always verify eligibility before choice filling.
