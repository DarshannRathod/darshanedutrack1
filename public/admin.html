📧 EmailJS OTP Configuration

Configure real-time OTP email delivery for students and counsellors.

📋 Setup Steps (5 minutes)

  1. Go to emailjs.com → Sign up free (200 OTPs/month)
  2. Add Email Service → connect Gmail (edutracksolution@gmail.com)
  3. Create two templates — one for Counsellors, one for Students
  4. Template variables: {{otp}} {{to_email}} {{user_name}} {{expiry}}
  5. Account → General → copy Public Key · paste below → Save
