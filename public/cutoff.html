EduTrack Tip

How to use cutoff data for choice filling

📊

Compare 3 years

A single year can be an outlier. Always use 3-year average closing ranks — if a cutoff dropped suddenly in 2023 but rose back in 2024, the 2023 dip was a fluke.

🎯

Add 10–15% buffer

If the 3-year avg closing rank is 10,000 and your rank is 9,500 — it's NOT safe. Always add a 10–15% buffer above the closing rank to consider a seat "safe".

🔄

Round 1 vs Round 2

Round 2 closing ranks are always higher (more seats filled). Use Round 2 data for planning. Round 1 ranks are lower and lead students to false confidence.
