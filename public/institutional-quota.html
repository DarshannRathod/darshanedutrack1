What is Institutional Quota?

Understanding Institutional Quota (IQ)

A special category of MBBS seats that colleges can fill directly, outside of state merit counselling rounds.

15%
of total MBBS seats in private
medical colleges — Institutional Quota
⚠️ Key Point: In Maharashtra, Institutional Quota is specifically 15% of total sanctioned seats in private unaided medical colleges. These seats are NOT filled through DMER / CET Cell counselling — colleges manage this process directly under regulatory oversight. EduTrack is an expert in IQ admissions across Maharashtra and other states.

📖 IQ — Quick Overview

  • Also called "Management Quota" or "B-Category" in some states
  • Available in: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, AP/Telangana, UP, Rajasthan
  • Maharashtra: 15% IQ out of total seats (e.g., 150 seat college = ~22 IQ seats)
  • Filled by the institution itself after state quota rounds are complete
  • NEET UG qualifying score is mandatory (no separate IQ NEET)
  • Fees are typically higher than state merit but lower than NRI quota
  • No domicile restriction in most states for IQ category
  • College directly issues admission letters after fee payment

Eligibility

Who Can Apply Under Institutional Quota?

✅ IQ Eligibility Criteria

  • Valid NEET UG 2026 qualifying score (above cut-off percentile)
  • Indian citizen (any state — no domicile restriction for IQ)
  • Age 17 years or above as on 31 December of admission year
  • 10+2 passed with Physics, Chemistry, Biology + English
  • Minimum 50% marks in PCB for General category (40% for SC/ST)
  • No upper age limit (unlike earlier rules)
  • No restriction based on previous NEET attempts (as per NMC)
⚠️ Who CANNOT apply: Students who have already been admitted to a government or private medical college and occupying a seat cannot apply for IQ unless they resign first. Students who forfeited a seat in state counselling may have restrictions.

📄 Documents Required

  • NEET UG 2026 Scorecard + Rank Letter
  • 10th Mark Sheet + Passing Certificate

  • Birth Certificate / School Leaving Certificate

  • PWD Certificate (if applicable)

  • Medical Fitness Certificate
  • Domicile Certificate (for Maharashtra specifically)
  • Gap Certificate (if gap year in education)

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Institutional Quota

Is IQ seat different from Management Quota?
They are often used interchangeably but technically different. In Maharashtra, "Institutional Quota" (IQ) specifically refers to the regulated 15% seats that colleges fill outside state counselling, under government oversight. "Management Quota" is a broader term used in Karnataka and other states. The common factor is that the college fills these seats directly (not via state merit counselling) but must follow NEET eligibility and fee regulations.
Can I apply for IQ if I already have a state merit seat?
Generally, you cannot hold two seats simultaneously. If you have a state merit seat and want to switch to IQ, you must resign from the state merit seat first. EduTrack strongly advises careful consideration before resigning as the state merit seat is usually more cost-effective.
When does IQ admission start in Maharashtra?
In Maharashtra, IQ admissions typically begin in September after DMER completes Round 2 of state merit counselling. The exact schedule is announced by DMER and individual colleges. Start document preparation in July–August to be ready. EduTrack alerts registered clients about IQ opening dates.
Is there any NEET cut-off for IQ seats?
Yes. The college sets its own IQ merit cut-off based on NEET scores within the IQ candidate pool. Typically, IQ cut-offs are lower than state merit cut-offs for the same college since fewer candidates compete. EduTrack has historical cut-off data for major IQ colleges.
Can students from other states get IQ seats in Maharashtra?
Yes! This is one of the major advantages of IQ seats. Maharashtra's IQ category is open to students from ALL states — no Maharashtra domicile required. This makes Maharashtra an attractive option for students from states with high competition like UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, etc.
What fees are required at the time of IQ admission?
Typically the first year's tuition fee is required within 24–72 hours of seat allotment. This ranges from ₹12–25 Lakhs in Maharashtra private colleges. Some colleges also require a refundable security deposit. EduTrack provides a complete fee breakdown for each target college before you apply.
Is IQ a legitimate route? Are there any legal issues?
Yes, Institutional Quota is a fully legal and government-approved category under the regulations of respective state medical councils and NMC. It was established specifically to give private colleges some autonomy in admissions while maintaining NEET merit. Fees are regulated by state fee committees.
