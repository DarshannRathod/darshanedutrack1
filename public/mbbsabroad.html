Why MBBS Abroad?

When India MBBS isn't reachable — abroad is the answer

India has 23+ lakh NEET aspirants competing for ~1,18,000 MBBS seats. Government seats are extremely limited. Private college fees are ₹50L–1Cr+. MBBS abroad is a genuine, NMC-approved alternative.

⚠️

NMC Guidelines — Know Before You Go

The NMC announced in November 2024 that medical degrees from international universities that do NOT follow NMC guidelines will not be valid for FMGE/NEXT in India. Always verify that your chosen university is NMC-approved before paying any fees. EduTrack only recommends NMC-approved universities — this is our commitment to every student.
