Understanding NRI Quota

What is NRI Quota in MBBS?

A reserved category of seats in private medical colleges for eligible NRI students and their relatives.

📖 NRI Quota — Key Facts

  • 15% of total MBBS seats in private/deemed colleges are reserved under NRI quota
  • Seats available in private unaided, deemed, and minority medical colleges
  • No state domicile restriction — pan-India eligibility
  • Fees are higher (typically USD 25,000–80,000 per year) but process is faster
  • NEET UG qualifying marks are required; cut-off is generally lower than state/AIQ
  • Governed by respective state counselling authorities (DMER, DME, etc.)
  • Both NRI students AND Indian students sponsored by NRI relatives can apply
⚠️ Important: NRI Quota rules vary state by state. Some states (like Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana) have specific NRI quota guidelines. Always verify current norms with the respective state counselling body. EduTrack provides updated state-wise guidance.
👨‍👩‍👧

Who Can Apply Under NRI Quota?

NRI candidates themselves, OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) cardholders, PIO (Person of Indian Origin) cardholders, and Indian students whose parents/siblings/blood relatives are NRIs (NRI-Sponsored category).

📊

Seat Availability

15% of sanctioned intake in private unaided medical colleges. In a 150-seat college, approximately 22–23 NRI quota seats are available. Deemed universities typically have higher NRI quota allocations.

🏦

Fee Payment

Fees are typically paid in USD (US Dollars) from a foreign bank account or in INR equivalent. Fees must usually be paid annually in advance through specified channels like the college's foreign currency account.

Eligibility Criteria

Who is Eligible for NRI Quota?

Both genuine NRI students and Indian students with NRI sponsors can apply. Check your eligibility below.

✈️

Direct NRI / OCI / PIO

  • Indian citizen residing abroad for work/business for at least 182 days/year
  • OCI cardholders (Overseas Citizen of India)
  • PIO cardholders (Person of Indian Origin)
  • Must have valid NEET UG qualifying score
  • Must have passed 10+2 or equivalent with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Age: 17 years as on 31 December of admission year
🤝

NRI Sponsored (Indian Resident)

  • Indian student whose sponsor (parent/sibling/close relative) is an NRI
  • Sponsor must have lived abroad for minimum 2+ years
  • Valid NEET UG score is mandatory
  • Must submit Affidavit of Relationship + NRI sponsor's documents
  • Sponsor's employment/residence proof abroad required
  • NRI sponsor's Foreign Bank Statement showing financial capability
📝

Academic Eligibility

  • Qualified NEET UG 2026 (compulsory from 2019)
  • 50th percentile for General; 40th for OBC/SC/ST (NEET qualifying %ile)
  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology as core subjects
  • Minimum 50% marks in PCB for General; 40% for reserved categories
  • English as medium of instruction preferred (in some states)
🏛️

State-wise Variations

  • Maharashtra: DMER conducts NRI quota counselling; seat matrix published separately
  • Karnataka: UGCET/COMEDK has NRI quota rounds; many deemed universities
  • Telangana/AP: DMHO/DMER NRI counselling; high competition
  • Tamil Nadu: TNMGRMU handles NRI quota for deemed institutions
  • Delhi/UP: Limited NRI quota in select private colleges

📄 For NRI Candidate (Self)

  • Passport (valid, with NRI stamps / work visa / residence permit)
  • NEET UG 2026 Admit Card + Score Card / Rank Letter
  • 10th and 12th Mark Sheets + Passing Certificates
  • Birth Certificate / School Leaving Certificate

  • Relationship Affidavit (notarised) proving relationship between student and NRI sponsor
  • Ration Card / Aadhar Card showing family relationship
  • NRE/NRO account details of sponsor
  • NRI status certificate (Embassy-attested in some states)
  • Undertaking for fee payment in USD / foreign currency

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — NRI Quota

Is NEET compulsory for NRI Quota MBBS?
Yes, absolutely. Since 2019, NEET UG is mandatory for all MBBS admissions in India — including NRI quota seats. Both NRI candidates and NRI-sponsored Indian students must have a valid NEET UG qualifying score to apply.
Can an Indian student whose uncle is NRI apply under NRI quota?
It depends on the state. Many states allow NRI-sponsored admissions where the sponsor can be a parent, sibling, or close relative (uncle/aunt in some states). The relationship must be proven through notarised affidavit and supporting documents. EduTrack can verify eligibility for your specific case.
How much lower is the NRI quota cut-off compared to general seats?
NRI quota typically has lower NEET score requirements than state merit/management quota but higher fees. For instance, while a government college requires 550+ NEET score, the same college's NRI quota may allot at 450+. Cut-offs vary by college and state — contact EduTrack for current-year NRI cut-off data.
Can fees be paid in Indian Rupees for NRI Quota?
Most colleges require NRI quota fees to be paid in USD through a foreign bank account (NRE/FCNR). However, some states like Maharashtra allow INR payment equivalent to the USD amount. Check with individual colleges and EduTrack for the latest rules before applying.
What if I don't fill the NRI quota seat and it goes vacant?
Vacant NRI quota seats (after all rounds) are often converted to management quota and given to Indian residents at INR fees. This sometimes provides an opportunity for Indian students at lower fees in later rounds. EduTrack monitors these openings and alerts clients.
Is there any reservation (SC/ST/OBC) within NRI quota?
Generally, NRI quota seats are treated as a separate category with no reservation for SC/ST/OBC within that category. All NRI quota seats are filled on merit (NEET score) within the NRI/NRI-sponsored pool.
How long does the NRI quota process take?
From NEET results (July) to final admission (September–October), the NRI quota process takes 2–3 months. Document preparation should ideally start 1–2 months before NEET results. EduTrack recommends starting the process in April/May itself.
